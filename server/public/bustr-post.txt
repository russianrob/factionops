<p><a href="https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/582927-bustr-busting-reminder-pda">BUSTR: Busting Reminder + PDA (updated)</a></p>

<p>It's the change straight from the diff: removed <code>calcTenHours</code> and swapped the decay from the exponential <code>128 / 2^(hours/7.2)</code> to Nosy's multiplicative inverse <code>128 / (1 + hours/10)</code>, in both the penalty score and the threshold. I replayed my own bust log through the old and new versions to double-check it lands on Nosy's numbers — for me it moved an available-busts count from 10 (old) to 16 (corrected), entirely from the threshold, which is exactly the schedule-shift case you described.</p>

<p>Heads up for anyone updating: your figures will jump. Older busts now keep counting much longer, so both your current penalty and your threshold rise. If you'd set a custom penalty threshold, clear it — it was calibrated to the old (wrong) curve; the automatic one recalculates itself. And ease into the higher available-busts number rather than maxing it straight away until you've sanity-checked it.</p>
