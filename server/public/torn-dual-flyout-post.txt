<h2>Torn Dual Flyout</h2>
<p><em>Torn's slide-out menu — on the side you want, as a compact icon rail you can scroll behind</em></p>
</div>

<p>Torn already has the mobile slide-out menu. This script doesn't add that — it just lets you decide <strong>how it behaves</strong>: which side it comes from, whether it's icons or text, and whether it stays open while you scroll.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Pick your side</strong> — have the menu open on the <strong>left</strong> or the <strong>right</strong>, whichever suits how you hold your phone. Swipe left-to-right for the left, right-to-left for the right; a small ◀ / ▶ under the menu button shows the current side, and it remembers your choice between page loads.</li>
<li><strong>Icons only</strong> — hide the menu names for a tight, one-handed icon column instead of a wide text list.</li>
<li><strong>Sticky</strong> — keep the menu open while you scroll the page, like a pinned sidebar — it even stays open as you tap through to other pages.</li>
</ul>

<p>Toggle icons-only and sticky from the little ⚙ next to the menu button. It also stays out of the way of the top bars — scrolling the icon and status rows won't set it off.</p>

<p>Nothing to set up and no API key needed. Built for Torn mobile — works in Torn PDA and mobile Tampermonkey.</p>
