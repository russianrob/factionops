<h2>Torn Dual Flyout</h2>
<p><em>Swipe to open Torn's mobile menu — on whichever side you like</em></p>
</div>

<p>On mobile, Torn's slide-out menu opens from the little hamburger button up in the corner. One-handed on a big phone that's an awkward reach, and it's always on the same side whether that suits your grip or not.</p>

<p>This script lets you just <strong>swipe to open the menu</strong> — and pick which side it comes from.</p>

<ul><li><strong>Swipe to open</strong> — swipe on the page instead of reaching for the menu button</li><li><strong>Two-way</strong> — swipe right-to-left and the menu opens on the <strong>right</strong>; swipe left-to-right and it opens on the <strong>left</strong>. Use whichever side fits how you hold your phone</li><li><strong>Side arrow</strong> — a small ◀ / ▶ under the menu button shows which side it's set to</li><li><strong>Remembers your side</strong> between page loads, so it stays where you left it</li><li><strong>Swipe back toward the edge to close</strong></li></ul>

<p>It stays out of the way of the top bars, too — scrolling the icon and status rows at the top won't accidentally trigger the menu.</p>

<p>Nothing to set up and no API key needed. Built for Torn mobile — works in Torn PDA and mobile Tampermonkey.</p>
