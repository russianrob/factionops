Lend Rob a US connection

He's in Italy and his US streaming won't play from there. You can fix it in about ten minutes by letting his phone borrow your internet connection. No cost, nothing to configure, and you can switch it off whenever you like.

What you'd be doing

Installing a free app called Tailscale on something at your home that stays on — a computer, or an Apple TV. His devices then route through your connection, so the streaming service sees an American address instead of an Italian one.

What this does and doesn't mean. His traffic passes through your internet connection, so it counts toward your data and uses a little of your bandwidth while he's watching. You can't see what he's browsing — it stays encrypted. He can't see anything of yours, and he has no access to your files or your network. Turn it off any time and nothing of yours changes.

Best device to use

An Apple TV is ideal — always on, always plugged in, nothing to remember. A desktop computer that stays on works just as well. A laptop works if you stop it sleeping. Avoid a phone; it'll drop off.

Setting it up

  1. Rob sends you an invite link to his Tailscale network. Open it and sign in (Google or Microsoft account is fine — no new password).
  2. Install Tailscale on the device you picked:
    • Apple TV — App Store, search "Tailscale"
    • Mac / Windows — tailscale.com/download
  3. Sign in with the same account you used for the invite.
  4. Turn on the sharing bit:
    • Apple TV — open Tailscale, go to Exit Node, choose Run as Exit Node
    • Mac — menu bar Tailscale icon → Exit Node → Run as Exit Node
    • Windows — right-click the tray icon → Exit Node → Run as Exit Node
  5. Tell Rob it's on. He approves it from his side and that's it.
That's everything. Leave the device on and plugged in. Nothing else to do, no settings to maintain, and it keeps working without you touching it.

If you want to stop

Open Tailscale, turn off Run as Exit Node, or quit the app entirely. It stops immediately and nothing else on your machine is affected.

Something not working?

Tell Rob which step you got stuck on and what you saw on screen. Almost always it's either not signed into the same account, or the exit-node switch not saved.
