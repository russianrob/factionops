<h2>RW Pricer — forums and trades</h2>
<p><em>Two new places it works</em></p>
</div>

<p>Most RW trading happens in forum threads, which was the one place the script couldn't see. It can now.</p>

<p><strong>Forum threads</strong> — it reads the stock people post, whether that's a screenshot or a typed-out list, and puts a price next to each item. Hover a price and it tells you what it matched on and how many sales that came from. Nothing to set up, no API key.</p>

<p><strong>Trades</strong> — the total at the top of a trade is now the RW price instead of Torn's value, with Torn's number in brackets so you can see the gap. The Steyr AUG below comes out at $1.07b. Torn had it at $74,589.</p>
