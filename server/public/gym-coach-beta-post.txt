<h2>Gym Coach Beta</h2>
<p><em>A coach on the gym page: what to do with this bar, and when your goals actually land</em></p>
</div>

<p>The gym page gives you four stats, some dots, and a bar. Everything else is on you. Which stat today, whether this gym is even the right one, whether to wait for the bar to fill, whether that can is worth what it costs, and when you'll finally hit the number you've been chasing. Most of us run all of that in our heads and get it slightly wrong for months.</p>

<p>This puts a small overlay on the gym page that does the arithmetic and gives you a verdict. Three tabs.</p>

<ul><li><strong>Now</strong> tells you one thing to do. "Train Defense here." "Wait 23m for full energy, then train Strength." Or, when you've wandered into a gym that can't train the stat you're on, "Don't train here" with where to go instead. Under it sits a cooldown rail for your drug and booster timers, with the bits of the rules people forget (a can still works at 46h of a 48h cap, because Torn only needs you under it)</li><li><strong>Plan</strong> is your goals per stat, each with a finish date. Tap the arrow to train a stat earlier and the list redraws in the order it will actually run. Gym unlocks get their own date, and a stat your current gym can't train gets a date at a gym that can. If you've locked a specialist gym, the plan stops walking you out of it</li><li><strong>Settings</strong> is your key, your goals, and your energy sources: the cans you hold, read from your inventory rather than a fixed list</li></ul>

<h3>The numbers correct themselves</h3>

<p>Most gym calculators tell you a date and never check whether they were right. This one keeps two figures from your own last 14 days. The gain model is what you really gained divided by what the maths predicted for the energy you really spent. Usage is the energy that reached the gym as a share of the energy that passed through your bar. Every ETA is multiplied by both, so a bar you keep leaving full shows up as days added to your goal instead of a number with no consequence. Below seven qualifying days it stays out of the way and says so.</p>

<p>There's also a <strong>Worth it?</strong> card. For the goal you're on right now, it asks what one more can a day would buy you, or one more xanax, and ranks them by cost per day saved rather than by sticker price. Prices are the cheapest live bazaar or item market listing. Refills are points and Mc Smoogle is capital you keep, so neither gets ranked as spend.</p>

<p>Smaller things it does along the way: it works out which stat book you're reading and shows the real countdown. It tells you what training at low happiness is costing you, as stat you didn't gain. In Torn PDA it pings you when the bar is full and when a happy jump window opens.</p>

<h3>Where your data goes</h3>

<p>Nowhere. There's no server behind this. The energy ledger that drives the calibration lives on your device and stays there. The script talks to api.torn.com for your stats and bars, and to weav3r.dev's public price mirror for the Worth it? card, where the only thing that leaves your browser is an item ID. Nobody, me included, can see how you train.</p>

<p>It needs your API key. A Limited key does most of it. A Full key lets it read your log, which is what the xanax count, the book detection and the finer calibration are built on.</p>

<h3>Why "Beta"</h3>

<p>Because the calibration is still being checked against real people's training, and the recent fixes have come from users. The latest one: a goal reporting "not reachable at this rate" when it was perfectly reachable. Eddie877 found it and found the trigger too. If a number looks wrong to you, it might be, and I want to hear about it.</p>

<p>Works on desktop with Tampermonkey and in Torn PDA.</p>
