Pre-war scouting coverage curves from FFScouter activity

How a faction actually behaves during wars — their own past war windows, hour by hour. A baseline curve measures a faction at rest and nobody fights at rest: checked against war 49287, both sides ran far above baseline and the baseline's top-ranked hour was not the war's best. Note that coverage is presence, not damage — Dead Fragment was out-covered in 26 of that war's 34 hours and won 23,048 to 7,923.

Leave the faction blank to scout whoever you are currently at war with. Key stays in this browser. Access is checked server-side against your faction position. FFScouter allows 10 requests a minute across everyone, so the first scout of a faction takes up to a minute. Finished wars never change and are cached permanently, so it is instant after that.
