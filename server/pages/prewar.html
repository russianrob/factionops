War Scout

FFScouter × Torn

How a faction behaves during wars — their own past war windows, hour by hour.

Method & caveats

A baseline curve measures a faction at rest, and nobody fights at rest. Checked against war 49287, both sides ran far above baseline and the baseline's top-ranked hour was not the war's best — so war windows lead here and the baseline is kept only as a hint.

Coverage is presence, not damage. Dead Fragment was out-covered in 26 of that war's 34 hours and won 23,048 to 7,923. Nothing here predicts a winner; it describes a shape.

pp = percentage points. Every figure here is a percentage of a roster, so the gap between two of them is quoted in points: 41% against 31% is +10pp. "10% better" would be ambiguous — 10% of 31% is barely 3 points.

Fade = last third of the war minus the first. Negative means they spiked and faded, positive means they built. A large negative fade in a SHORT war usually means the war was already decided and people stopped bothering, not that the faction collapsed — which is why the assessment only reads fade from wars past 20 hours. Not tracked means FFScouter had not begun collecting when that war was fought — roughly before April 2026.

FFScouter allows 10 requests a minute across all users, so the first scout of a faction takes up to a minute. Finished wars never change and are cached permanently.
