How far can we climb?

How this works. Pace is the respect gained between Torn's daily snapshots over the window, shown per week. Projections assume every faction keeps its pace and start from last midnight's standings; the table uses live respect. Most of our respect arrives with each war, so a pass date a few days out can land a few days either way. Positions follow Torn's Hall of Fame, which leaves out a few factions that appear in Torn's data.
